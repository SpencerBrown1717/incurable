Our pledge

Five lines. Hold us to all of them.

  • Only with experts

    Named investigators choose what gets tested.

  • Only through hospitals

    Their ethics review, their duty of care.

  • Safety before speed

    No route around consent or review.

  • Everything published

    The failures too.

  • We do not stop

    Until incurable covers less than it did last year.

A company, run like a cause

Incurable? is a Delaware C corporation, not a nonprofit. Curing a disease costs more than grant cycles can carry, so we built something that can raise what the work actually needs. The five lines above are what govern us, and they do not bend for a quarter.

How the AI helps

It reads. It ranks. It never decides.

  1. It reads everything

    Every paper, trial and filed away result on one disease.

  2. It ranks what is testable

    Sorted by what a hospital could safely try next.

  3. Clinicians decide

    They choose. We fund the work where we can.
